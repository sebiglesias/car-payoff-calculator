Frequently Asked Questions

Does making extra payments go directly to principal?

Yes, but you must ensure your auto loan servicer applies extra funds specifically toward the 'Principal' balance rather than treating it as prepaying the next month's interest.

Is there a penalty for paying off a car loan early?

Most modern US auto loans (from banks, credit unions, and captives like Toyota Financial or Ford Credit) have no prepayment penalties, but check your contract for 'Rule of 78s' or precomputed interest terms.

Should I pay off my car loan early or invest?

Compare your APR to current risk-free rates. If your auto APR is above 6-7%, paying it off delivers a guaranteed, tax-free return equal to your interest rate.
